DVNT DRLNG

Melbourne-based artist, producer and performer working across drum & bass, rap, live loops and creative performance.

ARTIST

DVNT DRLNG is the project of Melbourne-based artist Arwyn Carr, combining bass music, rap, beatboxing, live performance and improvisation.

The project is built around high-energy performance, audience interaction and a willingness to push outside conventional genre boundaries.

EXPERIENCE

With the focus always being on creating a strong relationship between performer, music and audience. DVNT DRLNG is a multi-disciplinary artist.

They have a wide range of experience DJing and performing live. In the early days, learning their craft as a busker, performing live across Australian cities. They developed a keen sense of reading the energy of a crowd, and curating their sound to match the vibe of the space and audience.

In recent years, stepping into the world of DJing and production. Playing across genres such as, tech house, deep house, garage, dubstep, before finding their home in DnB. They have performed at intimate bush doofs, house parties and self-organised pop-up dancefloors along the coast of Australia.

TECHNICAL

Technical requirements vary depending on the performance format and venue.

DJ →

DJ performances generally utilise venue-standard DJ equipment. Additional equipment can be discussed for smaller, alternative or unconventional spaces.

LIVE LOOP →

Gear for live performances is generally self-contained, with a small footprint and minimal setup time. While the artist can provide a soundboks speaker, PA system requirements vary depending on the space and audience size and larger spaces may require a venue-provided PA system.

WORKSHOPS →

Workshops are generally self-contained and acoustic. They are suited to 10-20 participants, but can be adapted for larger groups. They require a quiet space, with minimal background noise and an open area for participants to move and interact.
