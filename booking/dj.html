BOOKING / DJ

DJ

High-energy drum & bass journeys built around tension, filthy drops, unexpected turns and a strong connection with the crowd.

SOUND

DVNT DRLNG moves across rollers, jump up, liquid funk, neurofunk and dancefloor, with the freedom to shift direction as the room demands.

The goal isn't just to play tracks. It's to take the room somewhere — building a journey, finding the peaks, and creating moments that people remember.

EXPERIENCE

From small bush doofs and house parties to pop-up dancefloors on the coast, DVNT DRLNG has experience creating dancefloors in unconventional spaces and intimate environments.

Every room is different. The aim is to build a genuine relationship with the people in it and make the set feel like something happening together, rather than something being performed at them.

TECH

Venue-standard DJ equipment is preferred and expected for most bookings.

For smaller, unconventional or self-contained events, DVNT DRLNG can provide a compact setup including a Pioneer DDJ-GRV6 and portable sound system.
