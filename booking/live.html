BOOKING / LIVE PERFORMANCE

LIVE LOOPS

Live bass music built in the moment — layering, looping and manipulating sound to create something that doesn't exist until the room is there.

THE EXPERIENCE

Live Loops is a more unpredictable side of DVNT DRLNG. Instead of simply playing a finished track, elements are built, layered and transformed live.

Bass, rhythm, voice and texture can be looped, manipulated and thrown back into the mix, allowing the performance to respond directly to the energy of the room.

SOUND

IMPROV

Born from busking culture, DVNT DRLNG started out with nothing but a mic and a loop pedal. Skilled in on the spot thinking and audience interaction and participation.

RHYTHM

Beats, percussion and layered rhythmic loops all formed from natural elements in the room.

VOICE

Beatbox, vocals and manipulated sounds become part of the performance.

WHY LIVE?

Every performance can develop differently. Loops can be extended, broken apart, rebuilt or pushed somewhere unexpected depending on the space and the people in it.

It's designed for audiences who want to see something being created in front of them, rather than simply played back.

SPACES

Live Loops can work across intimate venues, alternative spaces, creative events, festivals and unconventional environments.

The setup can be adapted depending on the space, audience and technical requirements of the event.
