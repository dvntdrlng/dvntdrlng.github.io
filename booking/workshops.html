BOOKING / WORKSHOPS

RHYTHM OF BREATH

A creative, participatory workshop exploring rhythm, breath, beatboxing, improvisation and human connection.

THE IDEA

You don't need musical knowledge to make music. You already have rhythm, breath and a voice.

Rhythm of Breath uses beatboxing, vocal percussion, improvisation and group rhythm to make creative expression accessible to everyone.

WHAT HAPPENS

BREATHWORK

Using meditation breathing techniques and physical rhythm as the starting point for musical exploration.

BEATBOX

Turning simple sounds and vocal techniques into beats, patterns and rhythms.

IMPROV

Experimenting without worrying about getting it "right".

CONNECTION

Using call-and-response and group rhythm to listen, communicate and create together.

EVERYONE PLAYS

The workshop isn't about becoming a technically skilled beatboxer. It's about discovering that creative expression is already available to you.

Participants move from individual sounds and rhythms into shared musical patterns, learning to listen, respond and contribute to something larger than themselves.

TAKEAWAY

Rhythm of Breath is designed to build confidence, creativity and connection through something everyone already carries with them: breath, voice and rhythm.

BOOKING

Rhythm of Breath can be adapted for different groups, spaces and events.
